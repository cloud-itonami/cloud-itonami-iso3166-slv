# Operator guide — SLV

Portal: DINAC (Dirección Nacional de Compras Públicas, Ley de Creación
de la DINAC Decreto Legislativo No. 653), COMPRASAL electronic backbone
(Ley de Compras Públicas, Decreto Legislativo No. 652, 2023, which
replaced LACAP). Entity: Registro de Comercio (Código de Comercio,
Decreto Legislativo No. 671, dependency of the Centro Nacional de
Registros -- CNR) + NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria, Código
Tributario Decreto Legislativo No. 230) tax record. RUPES (Registro
Único de Proveedores del Estado) inscription is required before
participating in most compra pública processes (Art. 12; exceptions:
emergencia, urgencia, baja cuantía). A foreign-legal-entity bidder must
independently satisfy Ley de Compras Públicas Art. 24 literal f) --
validly constituted under its own country's law AND compliant with the
Salvadoran legislation applicable to its operation (Código de Comercio
Arts. 358-360: fixed domicile or sucursal, registered at the Registro
de Comercio) -- before a filing can proceed. Human-gated filing only.
